## How to publish

Add a `.md` file under `src/assets/blog` with a title, subtitle, and date at the top. The next time the site starts or builds, the post shows up here.

A short list of what the page keeps:

- Headings and paragraphs
- Lists like this one
- `inline code` and fenced blocks

```ts
const greeting = 'hello';
```

That is the whole workflow.
